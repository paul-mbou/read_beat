# Onboarding — ReadBeat

## Job
Teach the resume ritual, that files stay local, and ask once for lock-screen audio.

## Ritual
Four short desk pages → Skip anytime → Open my desk.

## Pages
1. Pick up the exact beat (Resume Pulse preview)
2. Files stay on this desk
3. Lock-screen audio needs a quiet yes (Allow / Not now)
4. Sign in only when you switch devices

## Avoid
Forced account, permission walls, gamified checklists.
